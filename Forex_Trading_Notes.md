## 1. Forex Basics: Currency Pairs

### Base Currency vs Quote Currency
In a pair like **EUR/USD**:
- **EUR** = Base Currency
- **USD** = Quote Currency

If EUR/USD = 1.10, then **1 Euro = 1.10 US Dollars**.

### Reading Market Direction
- If the market moves **up** → the **base currency (EUR)** is getting stronger.
- If the market moves **down** → the **quote currency (USD)** is getting stronger.

### Buy or Sell Decision
- If you think **EUR will be stronger than USD**, OR **USD will be weaker** → **YOU WILL BUY IT**
- If you think **USD will be stronger than EUR**, OR **EUR will be weaker** → **YOU WILL SELL IT**

## 7. Technical vs Fundamental Analysis

- **Fundamental analysis** = a "Mystery Box" — only about **50% good / 50% bad** reliability. Use Forex Factory to keep an eye on news, but **follow price action**, not news predictions.
- **Focus more on Price Action.**

> Price = Chart = The History
> Act(ion) = The Heart = The Fight

**Core idea:**
- Price action is what's needed to determine trends.
- **No indicator tells you the market trend.**
- The **market determines trend through its own structure.**
- Market structure = the "elbow"/turning points in price — these points are what determine the trend.

## 8. Market Structure (HH / HL / LH / LL)

- **Bullish structure** = **Higher Highs (HH)** + **Higher Lows (HL)**
- **Bearish structure** = **Lower Lows (LL)** + **Lower Highs (LH)**

### Updating Structure
- Once a candle's **body closes** beyond an existing HH, HL, LH, or LL, the market has shifted — you need to **relabel/update** your HH, HL, LH, LL points.
- Whenever a new HH forms, you will then be tracking the next HL that follows it (and the mirrored logic applies for LL → LH in a downtrend).
- Once price **breaks below the most recent HL** (in an uptrend) — or breaks above the most recent LH (in a downtrend) — that market is now considered to have flipped **bearish/bullish**.

### "Snake Trick"
A technique to identify the **last valid structure point** (the HL or LH):
- Trace back along the zigzag price line (the "snake") from the most recent LL (or HH) until the line **turns** — that turning point is your last valid structure point.

**Note:** Once you've identified the new market direction, mark fresh HH/HL (uptrend) or LH/LL (downtrend) points going forward.

## 9. Top-Down Analysis

**Core idea:** *"Trend is your friend."* Always read the market from higher to lower time frames.

### The 3-Tier Framework

| Time Frame | Role |
|---|---|
| Weekly (1W) | Trend + Area of Interest (Support/Resistance) |
| Daily (1D) | Trend + Area of Interest (Support/Resistance) |
| 4-Hour (4H) | Trend + Area of Interest (Support/Resistance) |
| 2H / 1H / 30M / 15M | Entry Signal |

**Method:** Identify the market structure (HH/HL or LH/LL) on **Weekly, Daily, and 4H** to determine the overall trend.

### How Far Back to Look
- **Weekly:** 5–6 years of price history to identify structure
- **Daily:** 1–2 years of price history to identify structure
- **4-Hour:** 6–12 months of price history to identify structure

### Time Frame Sync Rule
> **2 consecutive time frames must be in sync (agree on direction) for a pair to be worth trading.**

Examples:
- W↑ D↑ 4H↓ → ✅ Valid (Weekly & Daily, a consecutive pair, agree)
- W↓ D↑ 4H↑ → ✅ Valid (Daily & 4H, a consecutive pair, agree)
- W↓ D↑ 4H↓ → ❌ Not valid (no two consecutive time frames agree)

### Process
1. Use top-down analysis to determine if the pair is **bullish or bearish**, based on the trend time frames.
2. Once direction is determined, place HH/HL/LH/LL on **Weekly, Daily, and 4H** to map/trap price.
3. From this structure, identify the **Area of Interest (AOI)** — i.e., Support & Resistance / Demand & Supply / Order Block.

## 10. Area of Interest (AOI)

### What Makes a Valid AOI
- An AOI is only valid once it has been tested **at least 3 times** ("3 touches").
- A "touch" = a price reaction at either a support point or a resistance point.
- **The more touches, the better/stronger** the AOI.
- A valid AOI works for **both buying and selling**.

### Rules for Using AOI
- **You cannot take a trade unless price is at an AOI.** No AOI = no trade.
- The AOI must sit **within the current trend structure**:
  - It should not be higher than the current HH/LH, and should not be lower than the current LL/LH.
  - It should sit within either the HH–HL range (uptrend) or the LH–LL range (downtrend).

### Golden Rule
> **Buy = Support**
> **Sell = Resistance**

### Zone Size Limits
- **Maximum AOI zone width:** 60 pips (anything bigger is not valid)
- **Minimum AOI zone width:** 5 pips

### Discipline Rules
> "Wait for the work, don't make something work. If the market doesn't come to your area, switch to the next pair."

- If you can't find a valid Area of Interest, **wait for it** — don't force or invent one.
- **Only look for AOI on Weekly & Daily charts** — never on 4H.

## 11. Break of Structure / Break & Retest / Shift of Structure (CoCH)

Key terms: **Break of Structure**, **Break & Retest**, **CoCH (Change of Character)**, **Shift of Structure**.

- AOI should align with the HH/HL or LH/LL structure points.
- **Don't buy/sell in the middle of a move.** Let the market come back and retest the level, then trade.
- Take the trade on either the **upside of the AOI** (sell zone) or the **downside of the AOI** (buy zone).

### The Mechanics
1. Price breaks through your AOI.
2. Wait for price to come back and **retest** that broken AOI.
3. Enter the trade at the retest — **buy** if structure broke upward, **sell** if structure broke downward.

> **You can only enter a trade if a Break & Retest happens.**

## 13. Reversal Patterns: Head & Shoulders

- Forms at the **high** of the market structure: **Left Shoulder → Head → Right Shoulder**.
- **Inverted Head & Shoulders** forms at a **low** and signals price is about to go **up**.
- The pattern is identified using the market's structure (the HH/HL/LH/LL swing points) and can appear in several swing-point variations (labeled **H** for Head, **S** for Shoulder).

### Validity & Entry Rules
- **Valid only when price breaks the neckline.** A Head & Shoulders without a clear neckline break is **not valid**.
- **Sell at the break & retest of the neckline** (for a regular Head & Shoulders).
- **Selling at the right shoulder (before the neckline breaks) is extremely risky** — avoid this.
- **The neckline of a Head & Shoulders = a retest of the AOI.**
- *"Wait for the retest"* —
  - Only **sell** at the retest **after** the neckline breaks downward.
  - Only **buy** at the retest **after** an inverted Head & Shoulders' neckline breaks upward.
